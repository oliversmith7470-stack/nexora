The NEXORA Product — Ten phases. One creator-intelligence product.

NEXORA is being developed as a connected research and intelligence product for video creators. The ten phases describe the complete product scope. The public website explains these capabilities, while their application functionality belongs to the separate Phase 10 application.

Each phase below states what it does, who it is for, what the owner decides, and its truthful implementation status, verified against the NEXORA development record on 26 November 2026. The full test suite passed 1,754 of 1,754 tests, with 17 of 17 smoke checks, on that date. A description on this website does not mean that the corresponding application feature is live.

Phase 01 — Foundation

Purpose: Foundation establishes the product's starting point and operating scope. It provides the groundwork for the remaining NEXORA development phases.

Who it is for: The NEXORA owner and operator.

Owner's decision: Which channel and research objectives belong within the product's scope?

Where it operates: The Phase 10 application, where applicable. This public section is static documentation only.

Status: Design complete. Implemented and tested (project structure, configuration, dashboard).

Phase 02 — Research Engine

Purpose: The Research Engine is designed to study permitted public competitor videos and public performance information. It helps organise observations into research that can support video-idea discovery and creator decisions.

Who it is for: Creators researching existing content and potential opportunities.

Owner's decision: Which research findings are relevant and worth investigating?

Where it operates: The research functionality belongs in the Phase 10 application, not on this static page.

Status: Design complete. Implemented and tested (evidence collection, normalisation, quality assessment, AI research bridge, session orchestration — Stages 1–8).

Phase 03 — YouTube Intelligence

Purpose: YouTube Intelligence focuses on YouTube-related creator research. It supports the study of permitted public competitor videos and the intended use of the owner's authorised channel information.

Who it is for: YouTube creators.

Owner's decision: Which videos, subjects and research findings deserve attention?

Where it operates: The YouTube functionality belongs in the Phase 10 application.

Access limitations: NEXORA requests only yt-analytics.readonly and youtube.readonly.

Status: Design complete. YouTube research (milestone 3.1) implemented and tested. The Analytics collector (C3 Milestone 2) is built and under review, not yet committed. The Analytics collector is currently a stub; no live Analytics collection is operating.

Phase 04 — Facebook Intelligence

Purpose: Facebook is included in NEXORA's wider platform-research scope. This phase is intended to support research into relevant public Facebook video content.

Who it is for: Creators considering content opportunities across platforms.

Owner's decision: Whether a Facebook observation is relevant to the creator's content direction.

Where it operates: Any implemented Facebook research capability belongs in the Phase 10 application. This website provides documentation only.

Status: Design complete. Collection and normalisation (milestone 4.3) implemented and tested. The Facebook client and quota design (milestone 4.2) is design only — it awaits a live Meta session before it can be built.

Phase 05 — TikTok Intelligence

Purpose: TikTok is included in NEXORA's wider platform-research scope. This phase is intended to support research into relevant public TikTok content.

Who it is for: Creators researching short-form video opportunities.

Owner's decision: Whether a TikTok observation is relevant to a proposed video idea.

Where it operates: Any implemented TikTok research capability belongs in the Phase 10 application. This website provides documentation only.

Status: Design complete. Collection and normalisation (milestone 5.3) implemented and tested. The TikTok client and quota design (milestone 5.2) is design only.

Phase 06 — Opportunity Scoring

Purpose: Opportunity Scoring is designed to evaluate potential video ideas using a six-signal model. The purpose is to help the owner compare opportunities before deciding which idea to develop.

Who it is for: The creator evaluating competing video ideas.

Owner's decision: Which idea is worth pursuing?

Where it operates: The scoring functionality belongs in the Phase 10 application.

The six signals (from the approved Phase 6 design; the score is NEXORA's own model, out of 100, and is never presented as knowledge of a platform's private ranking algorithm):

SignalWhat it measures
Search demandEvidence of audience interest
Growth momentumWhether interest is increasing
CompetitionHow difficult it is to compete
EngagementPublic likes, comments and shares where available
Content freshnessWhether the topic is still relevant
Audience relevanceWhether the topic matches the intended audience

Weights: the signal weights are not fixed. Equal weights are the calibration baseline proposed in the approved design; the final values are settled by owner decision after compliance clearance. Search demand is built only from signals that actually exist — YouTube publishes no search volumes.

Important limitation: Independently calculated derived metrics are blocked until Google's written acceptance. No derived metric may be computed or stored before that acceptance.

Status: Design approved. Not yet implemented — the scoring modules are stubs. Operational scoring must not be claimed until its implementation and permitted operation are verified.

Phase 07 — Creative Studios

Purpose: The Creative Studios support the development of a selected video idea. The four studio areas are: Script Studio (supports the development of video scripts), Video Studio (supports video-production work), Thumbnail Studio (supports the development of video-thumbnail concepts), SEO Studio (supports video titles, descriptions and search-related preparation).

Who it is for: The creator developing an idea into a video package.

Owner's decision: The final script, visual direction, thumbnail and publishing information.

Where it operates: Studio functionality belongs in the Phase 10 application. This public page only describes the product scope.

Status: Design approved. Not yet implemented — the studio modules are stubs. The operational status of each studio must be verified separately. The studios use public-domain footage only, with no background music unless the owner explicitly allows it for a specific production.

Phase 08 — Publishing Manager and Autonomous Agent

Purpose: The Publishing Manager is intended to organise publishing preparation and drafts. The autonomous agent is designed to assist with supported workflows.

Publishing control: Drafts come first. Owner approval is required before anything is published.

Who it is for: The creator managing content preparation and publication.

Owner's decision: What is approved, what is published and when it is published.

Where it operates: The publishing functionality belongs in the Phase 10 application.

Status: Design complete. Implemented and tested (publishing manager and autonomous agent).

Phase 09 — Analytics Intelligence

Purpose: Analytics Intelligence is designed to examine the owner's own authorised YouTube Analytics information. It is intended to support private channel research and creator decisions.

Who it is for: The authorising channel owner.

Owner's decision: How to interpret the information and what changes to make to the channel.

Privacy: Owner-private Analytics are private to the authorising owner. They are not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or made available to anyone else.

Collection limits: The Analytics API collection is restricted to six non-monetary metrics. No revenue, CPM, demographic or thumbnail-impression data is collected.

Where it operates: Analytics collection and the real dashboard belong in the Phase 10 application. This static page does not collect or display Analytics.

Status: Design complete. Implemented and tested. The Analytics collector is currently a stub. No live Analytics collection is operating, and no derived metric has been computed or stored.

Phase 10 — Final Product

Purpose: Phase 10 is the NEXORA application release. It is intended to bring the product's research, intelligence, scoring, creative and publishing capabilities together.

Application environment: Cloudflare Pages and D1.

Who it is for: Initially, Shina as the single owner and tester.

Owner's decision: Which research findings to use, which ideas to develop, which drafts to approve and what actions to take.

Where it operates: In the separate Phase 10 application, not on the static GitHub Pages website.

Status: Design complete. Implemented and tested in the codebase. The public application is not yet deployed or open to other creators. Individual features must be described according to their independently verified implementation and testing status.

Product and website distinction

The NEXORA public website and NEXORA application serve different purposes. The public website is a static GitHub Pages home for product information, documentation, privacy and legal policies. The separate Phase 10 application is the runtime for working product functionality, using Cloudflare Pages and D1.
