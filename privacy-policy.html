1. Introduction

NEXORA is an owner-operated AI research and intelligence product for video creators. It is owned and operated by Shina (Omoyeni Shina John).

NEXORA is currently in development and single-owner testing. Its first and only YouTube channel in scope is ASHFALL, a dragon-fantasy storytelling channel.

This Privacy Policy explains the YouTube information NEXORA accesses, how that information is used, the separation between public competitor observations and the owner's private channel analytics, applicable retention periods, and the procedures for revoking access and requesting deletion.

NEXORA's public website is a static documentation website. The application functionality belongs to the separate Phase 10 application.

2. YouTube API Services

NEXORA uses YouTube API Services, including the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API.

NEXORA requests exactly two YouTube OAuth scopes:

  • yt-analytics.readonly — read-only access to non-monetary YouTube Analytics reports.
  • youtube.readonly — read-only YouTube account access, required for the Analytics API reports.query operation.

NEXORA does not request:

  • Monetary or revenue access scopes.
  • Write or modification scopes.
  • YouTube partner scopes.
  • Any additional YouTube OAuth scopes beyond the two listed above.

These permissions are intended for read-only creator research and channel analytics.

3. YouTube Analytics information collected

NEXORA's Analytics API collection is restricted to six non-monetary metrics:

  • views
  • estimatedMinutesWatched
  • averageViewDuration
  • averageViewPercentage
  • audienceWatchRatio
  • relativeRetentionPerformance

These metrics are accessed through the YouTube Analytics API reports.query operation.

NEXORA does not collect:

  • Revenue data.
  • Cost per mille (CPM) data.
  • Demographic data.
  • Thumbnail-impression data.

NEXORA does not request monetary Analytics access or use a monetary OAuth scope.

4. Owner-private channel analytics

The owner's own channel analytics are private to the authorising owner.

The first and only channel currently in scope is ASHFALL.

Owner-private analytics are used for the owner's private creator-research and decision-making activities.

Owner-private data:

  • Is not sold.
  • Is not shared with third parties.
  • Is not used for advertising.
  • Is not made available to anyone other than the authorising owner.

Public competitor observations are maintained separately from owner-private analytics.

NEXORA does not access competitor private analytics, estimate competitor private analytics, or represent unavailable private competitor information as fact.

5. Separation and non-aggregation

NEXORA does not combine owner-private analytics with public competitor information to produce mixed comparisons.

NEXORA does not perform cross-channel or cross-owner aggregation.

Public competitor observations and the owner's private channel analytics remain separate categories of information.

A public observation about another channel must not be represented as that channel's private Analytics report.

6. Independently calculated metrics

NEXORA distinguishes between information supplied by YouTube and information calculated independently by NEXORA.

Any metric NEXORA calculates itself, including sums, averages, ratios or custom scores, must be labelled as independently calculated by NEXORA.

Such a metric must not be presented as though YouTube directly supplied it.

Derived metrics are blocked until Google's written acceptance has been received.

Before that written acceptance, NEXORA does not compute or store derived metrics.

Following Google's written acceptance, derived metrics may be retained for up to 90 days, subject to the applicable requirements described in this Privacy Policy and the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

7. Data retention

NEXORA applies the following retention schedule:

Data categoryRetention period
Raw observations30 days
Derived metricsBlocked until Google's written acceptance; then 90 days
Public competitor statistics30 days until Google's written acceptance; then 90 days
Owner-private statistics90 days, subject to re-verification of authorisation every 30 days
Operational records90 days
Approval and audit records365 days

The restriction on derived metrics is a condition of the product's operation. NEXORA will not calculate or store derived metrics before Google's written acceptance.

Owner-private statistics are subject to authorisation re-verification every 30 days. If authorisation is revoked, collection stops immediately and the applicable deletion process begins.

The retention periods above are maximum retention periods for their respective categories, subject to earlier deletion requirements.

8. Revoking YouTube access

The authorising owner may revoke access to YouTube information.

Revocation may occur through NEXORA's in-application revocation process or through Google account security settings.

When access is revoked, collection stops immediately.

NEXORA will not use revoked credentials or tokens again.

The following deletion periods apply:

  • In-application revocation: Applicable data is deleted within 7 calendar days.
  • Revocation through Google security settings: Applicable data is deleted within 30 calendar days.

Revocation prevents further authorised collection. It also initiates the applicable deletion process for previously collected information.

9. User-requested data deletion

Privacy and deletion requests can be sent to: contact@nexora.is-a.dev

Following a valid user-requested deletion, applicable data is deleted within 7 calendar days.

Deletion covers the applicable information held by NEXORA under the request.

Deletion records retain identifiers and deletion metadata only.

They do not retain:

  • Metric values.
  • Private report contents.
  • Credentials.
  • Access tokens.

Deletion records are not a means of preserving deleted Analytics information.

10. Credentials and security

NEXORA applies strict handling requirements to credentials and access tokens.

Credentials and tokens are:

  • Never placed in Analytics records.
  • Never placed in application logs.
  • Never placed in tests.
  • Never printed.
  • Never hardcoded.

YouTube API Data is protected in accordance with the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

NEXORA does not treat credentials as Analytics information and does not include them in deletion records.

11. Google Privacy Policy and YouTube API policies

NEXORA's use of YouTube API Services is governed by Google's applicable requirements, including the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

Google's Privacy Policy is available here: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Users can review Google's policies and information about how Google handles information through its services.

The YouTube API Services Developer Policies govern NEXORA's handling of YouTube API Data, including applicable data use, retention, security and deletion requirements.
