About NEXORA — An owner-operated creator-intelligence project.

NEXORA is an AI research and intelligence product for video creators. It is owned and operated by Shina (Omoyeni Shina John). The product is designed to study permitted public competitor videos, research video ideas and evaluate opportunities to support creator decision-making.

The first channel in scope

ASHFALL is the first and only YouTube channel currently included in NEXORA's scope. ASHFALL is a dragon-fantasy storytelling channel.

Current development stage

NEXORA is in development and single-owner testing. It has no public accounts or external customers. It is not currently open to other creators.

A complete product in development

NEXORA is being developed across ten phases, from its foundation and research engine to opportunity scoring, creative studios, publishing and Analytics intelligence. Its public website documents the full product. The public website is static. The separate Phase 10 application is where working product functionality belongs.
