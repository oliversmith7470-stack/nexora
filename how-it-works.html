How NEXORA Works — Research. Understand. Evaluate. Decide.

NEXORA is designed to help video creators make informed content decisions through research and intelligence.

  1. Research public competitor videos: NEXORA studies permitted public competitor videos and public performance information. It does not access competitor private Analytics.
  2. Organise research findings: Relevant observations are organised to support an understanding of existing content and potential opportunities.
  3. Discover video ideas: Research findings help inform potential video-idea suggestions. The creator decides which subjects deserve further investigation.
  4. Evaluate opportunities: NEXORA is designed around a six-signal opportunity-scoring model. Independently calculated metrics are blocked until Google's written acceptance.
  5. Develop the selected idea: The Creative Studios are designed to assist with scripts, video production, thumbnails and SEO.
  6. Prepare publication: The Publishing Manager is designed to prepare drafts. Owner approval is required before publication.
  7. Review private channel intelligence: Analytics Intelligence is intended to use the owner's own authorised YouTube Analytics information. The Analytics collector is currently a stub, and no live collection is operating.

The creator remains in control

NEXORA supports research and decision-making. The owner decides what to research, which ideas to develop, what content to approve and what publishing actions to take. NEXORA does not guarantee video performance, views, rankings or audience growth.

Where the functionality operates

This page is static documentation hosted on GitHub Pages. Research, scoring, studios, publishing and Analytics functionality belong in the separate Phase 10 application using Cloudflare Pages and D1. The documentation website does not execute those functions.
