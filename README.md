# NEXORA — Public Website

Static GitHub Pages home for NEXORA, the owner-operated AI research and
intelligence product for video creators.

- Live site: https://oliversmith7470-stack.github.io/nexora/
- Custom domain (after the is-a.dev claim is approved): https://nexora.is-a.dev
- Contact: contact@nexora.is-a.dev

## Pages

- `index.html` — homepage
- `product.html` — all ten phases, with truthful implementation status
- `how-it-works.html` — the research process
- `about.html` — ownership and current status
- `privacy-policy.html` — full Privacy Policy
- `terms-of-service.html` — full Terms of Service

## Facts

- Plain static HTML. No database, no login, no OAuth, no engine, no images.
- The site documents the product; it does not run it. The working
  application is the separate Phase 10 release (Cloudflare Pages + D1).
- Privacy Policy and Terms of Service copy follows the approved ChatGPT
  draft (checked and adopted 26 November 2026). Effective date:
  26 November 2026.
- Phase implementation statuses on the Product page were verified against
  the NEXORA repository on 26 November 2026 (full suite 1,754/1,754 tests,
  smoke 17/17). Phases 6 and 7 are design-approved stubs, not implemented;
  the Analytics collector is a stub; no live collection is operating.
- The six opportunity-scoring signals and the equal-weights calibration
  baseline come from the approved Batch B Phase 6 design. Signal weights
  are not fixed — final values are settled by owner decision after
  compliance clearance.
