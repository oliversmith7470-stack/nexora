NEXORA
Research before you create.

NEXORA is an owner-operated AI research and intelligence product for video creators.

It is designed to study permitted public competitor videos and public performance information, suggest video ideas, and evaluate potential opportunities to support better creator decisions.

Explore the Product / How It Works

What NEXORA does

Competitor-video research: NEXORA studies permitted public competitor videos and public performance information to help the owner understand existing content and identify research opportunities. It does not access competitor private Analytics or present estimates of private competitor performance as fact.

Video-idea suggestions: NEXORA uses research findings to support the discovery and development of potential video ideas. The purpose is to help the creator identify subjects worth investigating before investing time in production.

Opportunity scoring: NEXORA is designed to evaluate video ideas using a six-signal opportunity-scoring model. The scores are intended to help compare potential opportunities. They are decision-support information, not guarantees of future performance. Derived scoring metrics remain blocked until Google's written acceptance.

How NEXORA works

NEXORA is designed around a straightforward research process:

  1. Research permitted public competitor videos and public performance information.
  2. Organise relevant research findings.
  3. Identify and suggest potential video ideas.
  4. Evaluate opportunities using the scoring model.
  5. Allow the owner to decide which ideas to pursue.
  6. Support production through the creative studios.
  7. Prepare publishing drafts for owner approval.
  8. Use the owner's private channel Analytics when the Analytics capability is operational.

The creator remains responsible for all final decisions.

The complete NEXORA product

NEXORA is being developed across ten phases.

  • Phase 01 — Foundation: The foundation of the NEXORA product and its operating scope.
  • Phase 02 — Research Engine: Research using permitted public competitor videos and public performance information.
  • Phase 03 — YouTube Intelligence: YouTube-focused research and intelligence.
  • Phase 04 — Facebook Intelligence: Facebook-related research within the product's intended platform scope.
  • Phase 05 — TikTok Intelligence: TikTok-related research within the product's intended platform scope.
  • Phase 06 — Opportunity Scoring: A six-signal model designed to evaluate potential video opportunities.
  • Phase 07 — Creative Studios: Script, video, thumbnail and SEO assistance for developing a selected idea.
  • Phase 08 — Publishing Manager and Autonomous Agent: Publishing preparation, draft management and owner approval before publication.
  • Phase 09 — Analytics Intelligence: Private intelligence based on the owner's own YouTube channel Analytics.
  • Phase 10 — Final Product: The application release that brings the product capabilities together.

Development and availability

NEXORA is in development and single-owner testing.

This website is the product's public documentation home. It is a static GitHub Pages website and does not run the research engine, Analytics collector or publishing functionality.

The separate Phase 10 application is the product runtime, based on Cloudflare Pages and D1.

The product's documented capabilities must not be interpreted as confirmation that every feature is operational.

The Analytics collector is currently a stub. No live Analytics collection is operating, no derived metric has been computed or stored, and no public statistic has been retained beyond 30 days.

NEXORA is not currently open to other creators.
